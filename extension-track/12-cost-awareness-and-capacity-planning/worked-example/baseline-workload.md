# Worked Example: Baseline Workload

## Scenario

A team runs a document-grounded answer generation workflow over a medium request set.

## Baseline Goal

Establish one-GCD throughput and completion quality before any larger allocation.

## Baseline Setup

- partition: LUMI-G
- resources: 1 GCD-equivalent task
- request set: 5k items sample
- outputs: responses, completion report, throughput summary

## Baseline Decision Question

Does baseline throughput meet pilot target with acceptable quality?
